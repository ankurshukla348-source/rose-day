My dearest Trinh,

I've been staring at my phone for hours trying to find perfect words, but the truth is simple and it hurts to say: I was wrong.

I said hurtful things. I made fun of you — the girl I love more than anything — and I turned my words, which should protect you, into something that made you feel small. There is no joke in the world worth your tears. Em không đáng phải nghe những lời đó, especially not from me.

You trusted me with your softest parts across thousands of miles, through laggy calls and late nights and “one more minute” that became three hours. And I was careless with that trust. I’m not here to explain it away with “I was tired” or “I didn’t mean it.” Impact matters more than intent, and I hurt you. I see that. I own that.

What I meant as teasing landed as disrespect. I hate that I made you — so kind, so funny, so strong — question for even a second how beautiful and loved you are. If I could take those words back, I would. Since I can’t, I will do the next best thing: change.

So here is my promise, not just pretty words: I will pause before I speak, especially when I’m joking. I will listen first when you’re hurt, not defend myself. Your feelings will never be the punchline again. And I will show you in small everyday ways that you are cherished — because you are.

543 days (almost) of us, all online yet so real. You’ve been my morning, my midnight, my home I haven’t hugged yet. Please forgive me, not because I deserve it instantly, but because I want to spend every day after this proving I’ve learned.

Anh yêu em — more than yesterday, more carefully tomorrow.
